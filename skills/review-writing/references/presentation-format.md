# Presentation format — review-writing

The presentation agent's response IS the report. Every section and finding appears in full; no summary, no truncation. Pass `Location:` through verbatim. Omit sections with no content, except Coverage.

The report follows the feedback model of Hattie and Timperley (2007): where the writing is going (feed up), how it is going (feed back), where to next (feed forward). It gives task-level detail in the findings and, where the findings allow, process-level next steps. It comments on the writing, never on the writer.

## Skeleton

```
[HEADER from assets/report-header-template.md]

### Where this draft is going
[From the argument agents' "Reading of the draft": the central claim as read, quoted, and what the draft seems to be trying to do, in two or three sentences. If the two agents' readings differ, show both: that difference is itself a finding about clarity of claim.]

### What is working
[2–3 specific strengths, chosen from all agents' Strengths sections; distinct passages. Format: > "[quote]" — [Domain]: [why this works].]

### Where it needs work
**Highest priority** — upheld findings with analysis HIGH and Severity High, then Medium. One line each: `Para [n] · [Domain] · [one-sentence summary]`. Cap 5; say how many more follow.
**All upheld findings by domain**, in order Argument structure → Evidence → Clarity → Voice → Cross-domain; within a domain High → Medium → Low, confirmed before partial-upheld. Per finding: Para, Current text (≤80 chars), Domain, Analysis confidence, Challenger verdict, Severity, Concern.
**By location**: for each paragraph with an upheld finding, a bullet list of its findings.

### Where to next
[Up to three next steps, ordered by the priority above. Each step names the finding numbers it resolves and asks for one concrete revision act, preferring process and self-checking moves over fixes to single sentences: "State the warrant that connects paragraphs 3 and 4 in one sentence, then check each later paragraph against it". Derive steps ONLY from upheld findings; add no new criticism here.]

### Needs your decision
[split + ambiguous + partial-ambiguous + partial-unaddressed; both verdicts or the challenger's question verbatim.]

### Disputed (for reference)
[confirmed-disputed + partial-disputed: `[Domain] para [n]: "[quote excerpt]" — disputed because [reason]`.]

### Unverified findings
[analysis UNVERIFIED with a confirmed challenger, grouped by domain; and any `[UNVERIFIED-CHALLENGER]` findings.]

### Coverage
Bucket mapping: upheld = confirmed + partial-upheld; disputed = confirmed-disputed + partial-disputed; needs your decision = split + ambiguous + partial-ambiguous + partial-unaddressed; unverified-challenger.
Argument structure: [n] upheld (HIGH [n], UNVERIFIED [n]) | [n] disputed | [n] needs your decision | [n] unverified-challenger
Evidence / Clarity / Voice: (same)
Cross-domain (merged): [n] entries
Total findings: [n] upheld | [n] disputed | [n] needs your decision | [n] unverified-challenger
```
