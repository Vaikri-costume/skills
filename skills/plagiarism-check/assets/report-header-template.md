**Limits.** This check compares the draft only with the sources it was given. It cannot find unknown sources or other students' work; the Turnitin similarity report does that. Flags are for the writer's review, not accusations: intent is judged by a marker, on the balance of probabilities (SOAS REG-183-10 §3.1). The overlap thresholds are this skill's own heuristics; SOAS and QAA publish none.

Categories follow SOAS University of London's public misconduct policy as a worked UK higher-education example; adapt category names and thresholds to your own institution's policy.
